Welcome to Poop Tracker

A tiny app with one job: keeping track of the days you poop.

  • 👆 Tap a day on the calendar to log a poop.
  • 💩 Logged days show a little poop instead of the date.
  • 🧻 Tap a pooped day again to remove it.
⚠️

Heads up

Your poops are not saved in a database. They live only in this browser, on this device.

If your browser storage gets cleared, or something gets corrupted, your poop history will be gone for good.
